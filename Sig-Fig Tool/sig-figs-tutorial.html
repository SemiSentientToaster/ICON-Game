Tutorial

  1. Counting significant figures:
    • Don't count the leading zeros. They are not significant.
    • Count all non-zero digits.
    • Count zeros trapped between or to the right of significant digits.
    • Don't count trailing zeros if the measurement has no decimal point.

  2. Math with Significant Figures
    • Adding / Subtracting:
      • The number of decimal places in the answer is equal to the lowest amount of decimal places between the previous measurements.
    • Multiplying / Dividing:
      • The number of significant figures in the answer is equal to the lowest amount of significant figures between the previous measurements.
